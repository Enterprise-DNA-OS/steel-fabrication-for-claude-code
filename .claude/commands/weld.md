---
description: Record who welded a mark and to which weld procedure (WPS). Warns when the welder is not qualified for the project's construction category.
---

1. Run `npm run steel -- weld <project> <mark> --welder=<code or name> --wps=<WPS code>`.
2. If it warns that the welder is not qualified, tell the operator plainly and suggest the welding coordinator review the weld before the mark moves on. Do not delete the record: it is the evidence.
