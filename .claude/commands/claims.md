description: Progress claims and where each stands, flagging any with no payment schedule in time.
---

Run `npm run steel -- claims --open`. For any flagged NO SCHEDULE IN TIME, explain that under the Construction Contracts Act the payer who does not give a schedule in time becomes liable for the claimed amount, and offer `/draft-overdue-claim`. Record what comes back with `claim-status <claim> --scheduled=<amount>` or `--paid=<date>`.
