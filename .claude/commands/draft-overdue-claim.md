---
description: Draft the follow-up on a payment claim that had no payment schedule in time.
---

1. Run `npm run steel -- claims --json` and pick the claim.
2. Write a factual note: the claim number, amount, date submitted, the date the schedule was due, and that no schedule has been received. Ask for payment of the claimed amount by a date.
3. Do not threaten or quote the Act beyond naming it. The operator decides on adjudication or recovery with their adviser.
4. Save to `drafts/claim-<number>.md`. Never send it.
